FAQ

Questions answered
before you ask

How long does it take to build my website?
+

Starter is delivered in 7 days, Pro in 14 days, Premium in 21 days. The clock starts once you've provided your content. We send a simple checklist on day one — no guessing, no back-and-forth.

What do I need to provide to get started?
+

Your logo, brand colors, photos, and the text for each page. Don't have those? No problem — we can recommend copywriters, use high-quality stock photography, and help establish your brand basics as an add-on. We've launched sites for clients who handed us nothing but a business name.

Do I own the website after you build it?
+

100%. You get full ownership of every file, asset, and line of code. We help you set it up on your own hosting so you're never dependent on us to keep the site live. No monthly fees, no lock-in, ever.

What if I want changes after launch?
+

Pro and Premium packages include 30–90 days of post-launch support. After that, we offer a simple maintenance plan, or we hand you the keys. We build on platforms that are easy to self-manage — no technical skill needed.

Why is your pricing public?
+

Transparency. These prices fit 90% of what clients need. If your project needs more, we'll tell you on the call — no pressure, no bait-and-switch. Public pricing filters out bad-fit projects before anyone wastes their time.
