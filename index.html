FAQ

Questions answered
before you ask

How long does it take to build my website?
+

14 days from the moment you provide your content. We send a simple checklist on day one so there's no guessing — just check the boxes and we handle the rest.

What do I need to provide to get started?
+

Your logo, brand colors, photos, and the text for each page. Don't have those? No problem — we can help with copywriting, use high-quality stock photography, and establish your brand basics. We've launched sites for clients who handed us nothing but a business name.

Do I own the website after you build it?
+

100%. Every file, asset, and line of code is yours. We help you set it up on your own hosting so you're never dependent on us to keep the site live. No monthly platform fees, no lock-in, ever.

What's included in the 250 AED/month maintenance?
+

Monthly content updates, security patches, uptime monitoring, backups, priority WhatsApp support, and a monthly performance report. It's completely optional — add it after your build, and cancel anytime with no penalty.

Why is your pricing public?
+

Transparency. We believe you should know what you're paying before you pick up the phone. Public pricing filters out bad-fit projects and saves everyone's time — including yours.
